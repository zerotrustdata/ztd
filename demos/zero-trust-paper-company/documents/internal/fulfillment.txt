INTERNAL — FICTIONAL WAREHOUSE FULFILLMENT PROCEDURE

Review the confirmed order and check SKU, selling unit and bin. Available stock equals on-hand stock minus reserved stock. An order reserves stock; loading a route reduces both on-hand and reserved quantities. Do not record an additional manual stock deduction for the same shipment.

Route capacity in this simulation counts selling units. Paper Plane (TRK-01) holds 120 units; Ream Machine (TRK-02) holds 80 units. Real weight, pallet dimensions and driving constraints are outside this fixture. Each truck and driver can take one route per day.

Only operations staff load an order. Drivers mark only their own shipments delivered. For Beacon Community School, verify the before-15:00 reception window with support. The dispatch board supplies delivery addresses; the public product catalog must not include them.

When stock is low, purchasing creates a purchase order from the supplier catalog. Receiving the purchase order adds stock once and records a movement. A manual adjustment requires a count discrepancy reason and must leave enough stock for existing reservations.
